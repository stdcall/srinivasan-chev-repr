# Representations of Finite Chevalley Groups

Bhama Srinivasan, *Representations of Finite Chevalley Groups: A Survey*,
Lecture Notes in Mathematics 764, Springer-Verlag, 1979.

The survey develops the representation theory of finite Chevalley groups,
from algebraic groups and Frobenius maps to principal series and cuspidal
representations, ℓ-adic cohomology, and the Deligne–Lusztig construction.

The edition uses searchable mathematical text, linked references, and a
subject index. Confirmed corrections are recorded separately.

PDF editions and their checksums are distributed through
[GitHub Releases](https://github.com/stdcall/srinivasan-chev-repr/releases/latest).
Local builds are available in `build/`. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)
for source conventions and build commands.
